Landwright

Read the land. Rebuild the map.

A map puzzle for Android. Every map hides its rivers, roads, towns, fields and forests. The hills, valleys and coastline are left showing, and they tell you where everything belongs. Put each piece back, hex by hex.

Coming soon to Google Play

How it plays

Look

Valleys show where rivers run. Contour lines show where the land climbs. Badges for cattle, fish, timber and ore show where people would settle.

Place

Drag a river, village, field or road from the tray onto the hex where it belongs, or tap a piece and then a hex.

Reveal

Each right piece brings back a little more of the real map, until the whole country is drawn.

A land with rules

Every hidden piece follows the same rules on every map, so you can reason your way to each one:

  • Rivers run downhill from high ground to the sea, a lake, or off the map.
  • Fields lie within two hexes of a river, away from the salty shore.
  • Forest covers the green flat land farther from the rivers.
  • Towns and cities rise on rivers and shores where resources gather.
  • Roads join every settlement along the route that climbs the least.

Four levels

Easy

Strong hints, glowing spots where a piece can go, and a card that explains why each settlement, field, forest and road belongs.

Normal

Softer hints and fewer clues on the map.

Expert

Faint hints, heavier fog, and pieces in the tray that are not on the map at all.

Master

Expert, with no resource badges to guide you.

Also inside

Tutorial

Four short steps teach the rules on one small map. Skip it any time.

Daily map

A new map every day, the same for every player on the same size and level.

Endless worlds

Four map sizes, from a quick island to a sprawling country, and a fresh world every time you tap Start.

Sound of the land

A relaxed jazz soundtrack, and sounds for rivers, waves, wind, livestock and markets as you move across the map.

Share your map

Send a picture of your finished map and your score to friends.

No data collected

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The game never connects to the internet.
