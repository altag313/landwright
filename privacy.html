Landwright Privacy Policy

Landwright, an Android map puzzle game by Liberato. Effective 24 October 2026.

Summary

Landwright does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information the app collects

None. Landwright asks for no permissions on your device and does not gather your name, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage data or any other information about you.

Information kept on your device

To work, the game keeps a few things in its own private storage on your phone:

  • the game in progress, so it can continue where you left off;
  • your best scores, for each map size and level and for each day's Daily map;
  • your settings: music and sound effects on or off, whether the tutorial has been seen, and whether rule tips are shown.

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling Landwright, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Sharing

When you tap Share at the end of a game, Landwright makes a picture of your map and a short message with the map's name, level and score, and hands them to Android's share sheet. They go only to the app you choose, and only when you choose to send them. What happens to them after that is governed by that app's own privacy policy. Landwright receives nothing back.

Third parties

Landwright contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or other third party code that collects data.

Children

Because Landwright collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
